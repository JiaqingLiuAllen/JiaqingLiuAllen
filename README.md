# Jiaqing (Allen) Liu

I’m a second-year PhD student in Computer Science & Engineering at the University of Notre Dame. My research follows two threads:

- **Multimodal AI for health:** foundation models that learn efficient representations from multimodal wearable data, and AI agents that reason over these signals for health monitoring and improvement.
- **Health sensing:** turning everyday objects (e.g., clothing, glasses, and necklaces) into wearable sensors that capture health signals unobtrusively and continuously in daily life.

### Selected research

- **ECG Necklace · CHI 2025** — A low-power wireless necklace that records an electrocardiogram during everyday wear. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/3706598.3713742)
- **Circuit2Yarn · CHI 2026** — We rolled thin, flexible circuits into yarn so that sensors and LEDs could be woven into fabric. [Project and videos](https://zhechenzhao.github.io/Circuit2Yarn-Project-Page/)
